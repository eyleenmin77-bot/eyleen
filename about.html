About Me ♡

I'm Eyleen, a Parsons MPS Fashion Management graduate with a USC background in sociology. I'm building my career in fashion and beauty, with a particular interest in the CPG and lifestyle products that become part of people's everyday routines.

I love figuring out why people connect with a brand, then turning that insight into something they can see and feel: a pop-up, a creator partnership, a thoughtful PR story, or a piece of social content. My experience in public relations, merchandising, and e-commerce helps me bring both creative ideas and the behind-the-scenes details together.
