About Me ♡

Hi I’m Eyleen, a Parsons MPS Fashion Management graduate with a background in sociology from USC. I’m drawn to fashion, beauty, and lifestyle brands because of the way they shape how people express themselves and connect with the world around them.

I love exploring what makes people connect with a brand and turning that insight into something memorable, whether it’s a pop-up, a creator partnership, a PR story, or social content. My experience in public relations, merchandising, and e-commerce has taught me how to bring creative ideas to life while taking care of the details behind them.
