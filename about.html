About Me ♡

I'm a fashion marketing professional drawn to the stories behind what people wear, use, and love. My work spans public relations, influencer marketing, content, and e-commerce, with a focus on fashion, beauty, and consumer lifestyle goods.

I enjoy connecting creative ideas with the details that make them happen: researching audiences, shaping a brand story, coordinating creators and media, and bringing campaigns to life. I'm especially interested in work that feels culturally aware, visually compelling, and genuinely useful to its audience.
