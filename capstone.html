Gen Z, Counterfeits, and the Price of Luxury

A cross-cultural study of consumer motivation in the United States and South Korea.

Research

Survey of 70 consumers ages 18–27 plus digital content analysis across TikTok and Reddit.

Key Finding

Price was the leading driver, while cultural differences shaped how counterfeit consumption was discussed and managed.
