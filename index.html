CAPSTONE.PDF

Graduate Capstone

Gen Z, Counterfeits, and the Price of Luxury

A cross-cultural study examining the motivations behind Gen Z counterfeit and dupe consumption in the United States and South Korea.

The study combined a survey of 70 consumers ages 18–27 with digital content analysis across TikTok and Reddit. Findings revealed that price was the leading purchase driver, while cultural differences shaped how counterfeit consumption was discussed and managed.
